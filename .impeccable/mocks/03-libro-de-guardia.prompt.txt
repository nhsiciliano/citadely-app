Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity paired product concept board for a responsive SaaS web app, landscape desktop presentation.
Primary request: Design one cohesive Citadely visual system shown as TWO full website screens side by side: a marketing landing page on the left and an authenticated operations dashboard on the right. Concept name: "Libro de guardia". This is a shippable product UI mockup, not concept art and not a poster.
Reference images: Image 1 is structural inspiration for a complete conversion landing page; Image 2 is structural inspiration for a detailed weekly schedule. Do not copy their branding, palette, exact cards, people, or content.
Product truth: Citadely is a "Central de turnos" for service businesses in Argentina. It centralizes agenda, services, clients, public booking and deposit status. No employees module. Landing must demonstrate the real product rather than stock photography.
Composition: 16:9 design board with landing screen on the left 56% and dashboard screen on the right 44%, separated by a narrow precise gutter. The visual driver is a contemporary shift log: a strong date spine, ruled rows, check marks and compact status annotations, translated into clean digital product UI rather than nostalgic paper. LANDING hero has a large headline across the top and beneath it a wide operational ledger that shows the complete journey of three synthetic appointments from public request to deposit to confirmed slot. The primary CTA is integrated into the ledger header. Continue with capabilities as labeled rows, industries index, public booking preview, FAQ and existing pricing. DASHBOARD has persistent midnight navigation and a ledger-like operational overview: date rail on the left of content, a dense table of today's appointments in the center, a narrow status and quick-action rail, and a partly open appointment details drawer. Include a week/day switch and compact service/status filters. High scanability, straight rules, few containers, hierarchy through spacing and ink rather than many cards.
Visual world: modern operational almanac / control ledger. Cool blue-gray page, white surfaces, midnight-blue navigation and headings, saturated cobalt action fields, restrained violet secondary accent, strong green/amber/red semantic annotations. Thin rules, tabular rhythm, square-to-small 6px corners, crisp borders, compact controls, no excessive pills. Funnel Display character for marketing headings; Geist for interface; Geist Mono for dates, times and metrics.
Exact prominent text: "Citadely", "Tu central de turnos", "Cada turno, bajo control", "Crear mi cuenta", "Jornada de hoy", "Jueves 4 SEP", "Estado", "Nuevo turno".
Use clearly synthetic Argentine names and services only as demonstration data. Do not add testimonials, company logos, fake commercial outcomes, awards, fake statistics or invented integrations.
Style/medium: premium realistic UI design presentation, pixel-sharp web product design, readable typography and practical controls.
Constraints: light theme only; no beige; no glassmorphism; no decorative gradients; no stock photos; no floating abstract 3D objects; no giant rounded cards; no repeated generic feature cards; no browser chrome or device mockup; no watermark.
